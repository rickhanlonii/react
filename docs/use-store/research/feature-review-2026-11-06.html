F1 An async action's store update committed before the action finished

Found by: Slice 1 (finding 4) and slice 2 (F1), independently. Refined by the fuzzer (finding 7).

The HostRoot only suspended on a pending async action when the store had no readers, and a reader only suspended when it had a previous read of the store. Hidden readers, readers inside a boundary already showing its fallback, and readers on their first read fell through both checks. The HostRoot then committed the action's value, and a reader that mounted urgently, or a hidden reader that was revealed, showed it early. With useReducer at the root, nothing commits until the action is done.

Fix: after the HostRoot processes its stores, it suspends if any store applied the pending action's update and its value differs from the committed value. Records first processed mid-render get the same check, before their state is kept on the HostRoot. The reader-side check could no longer be reached, so it's removed. The "value differs" part comes from the fuzzer's finding 7: useState only suspends when its new state differs from the state its Hook committed (ReactFiberHooks.js, updateReducerImpl), so a store mustn't suspend a reveal whose value a later update already made the committed one.

A SuspenseList together list renders a row's boundary twice, and the first pass took the slot. Either way the boundary committed its fallback without subscribing, and a store update never retried it. Context handles both cases.
